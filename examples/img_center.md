# Centered Image (`![center](...)`)

![center](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1506744038136-46273834b3fb?w=560&h=320&fit=crop)

Adding `center` to the image syntax (`![center](...)`) centers a standalone image horizontally on the slide while keeping surrounding text left-aligned.
